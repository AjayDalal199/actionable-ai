# Product Vision Board

## The Vision Statement
To become the standard infrastructure that empowers every software company to instantly deploy highly intelligent, action-oriented AI assistants, seamlessly bridging the gap between their codebase knowledge and their end-user experience.

## Target Group
*   **B2B SaaS Companies:** Looking to scale their customer support without proportionally scaling headcount.
*   **Engineering/Developer Teams:** Who are tired of manually writing and updating technical documentation and customer FAQs.
*   **Product Teams:** Who want to reduce user friction and increase feature adoption.

## Needs (The Problem)
*   Support documentation is always out of date with the actual codebase.
*   Standard chatbots are passive "answering machines" that frustrate users who just want to complete a task.
*   Building a custom agentic AI from scratch takes too much engineering time away from the core product.

## The Product
The **Actionable AI (A²I) Platform** automatically ingests knowledge from code repositories and allows developers to register frontend tools and backend APIs, turning a passive knowledge base into an active, agentic assistant.

## Business Goals
*   Establish a Developer-First (PLG) growth motion.
*   Achieve high retention through deep integration into the client's CI/CD pipeline and customer support workflows.
*   Upsell advanced features like Native Helpdesk Integrations (V2) and Multi-Language Support (V3).
