## 1. Feature Overview

### Purpose
To convert B2B visitors into active users by clearly communicating the value of A²I: secure, action-oriented AI agents.

### User Goal
Understand what the platform does, view the security features (Human-in-the-Loop), and sign up for an account.

### Success Criteria
- High conversion rate on the "Get Started for Free" CTA.
- Users understand that A²I is not just a chatbot, but an *actionable* agent framework with strict security guarantees.

---

## 2. Page Structure & Copy

### 2.1. Hero Section
*   **Headline:** Turn your API into an Actionable AI Agent.
*   **Subheadline:** Embed a fully autonomous, RAG-powered AI assistant into your product in minutes. Built for the enterprise with strict Human-in-the-Loop security guarantees.
*   **Primary CTA:** Get Started for Free (Routes to Dashboard Signup).
*   **Secondary CTA:** View Documentation.
*   **Visual:** A split-screen graphic showing a JSON API schema on the left, and the chat widget successfully executing an action on the right.

### 2.2. Feature Grid (Value Proposition)
1.  **Bring Your Own APIs:** "Simply paste your OpenAPI specs or JSON schemas. Our platform automatically maps them to LLM tools."
2.  **Instant RAG:** "Drag and drop your company policies or PDF manuals. Your agent learns your business context instantly."
3.  **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Security:** "Never worry about rogue AI. Flag destructive endpoints (like DELETE) to force a human confirmation card before execution."
4.  **Embeddable Shadow DOM Widget:** "Drop one script tag into your codebase. Our widget is perfectly isolated from your CSS, ensuring a flawless UI every time."

### 2.3. Footer
*   Links to: Pricing, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Contact Sales.

## 3. UI/UX & Design System Constraints
*   **Style:** Must strictly adhere to the `Enterprise_Design_System.md`.
*   **Colors:** Deep slate grays (`#0F172A`) for backgrounds, Enterprise Blue (`#2563EB`) for the CTA buttons.
*   **Visuals:** Flat design, NO gradients, NO glassmorphism, NO neon. High information density and professional typography.
