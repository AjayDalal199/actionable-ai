# User Research Plan

## Objectives
*   Validate the assumption that developers find manual documentation of new features tedious and error-prone.
*   Determine the level of trust developers and product managers have in AI auto-generating documentation from a codebase.
*   Understand the primary actions end-users wish a chatbot could perform *for* them on a typical SaaS platform.
*   Identify the friction points developers face when trying to implement agentic tool-calling with existing frameworks (like LangChain or CopilotKit).

## Target Audience
*   **Demographics:** Software Engineers (Frontend & Fullstack), Product Managers, Technical Support Specialists.
*   **Behavioral Traits:** Teams currently using GitHub/GitLab for CI/CD. Companies that currently use a customer support chatbot (e.g., Intercom, Zendesk) but find it lacking.

## Methodology
*   [x] User Interviews (1-on-1) - Target: 5-8 Developers, 3-5 Product Managers.
*   [x] Surveys/Questionnaires - Target: 50+ SaaS users regarding their chatbot experiences.
*   [ ] Observational Studies

## Key Questions (For Developers/PMs)
1.  Walk me through your current process for updating documentation when a new feature is merged into `master`.
2.  How often do users complain that your help center or chatbot is out of date?
3.  If an AI could read your codebase and generate a draft of the documentation, what would your review process look like? What are your primary security or accuracy concerns?
4.  Have you tried building a chatbot that can execute frontend functions (like navigating pages or clicking buttons)? If so, what was the hardest part?

## Key Questions (For End-Users)
1.  Think about the last time you used a chatbot on a website. What were you trying to do, and did the bot actually help?
2.  If you ask a bot "How do I change my billing cycle?", would you prefer it gives you a list of instructions, or provides a button right in the chat to do it for you?
3.  What is the most frustrating part of interacting with automated customer support?
